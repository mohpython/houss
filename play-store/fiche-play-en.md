## Full description

```
SAHA Santé brings your pharmacy to your home, in Bamako.

How it works:
1. Take a photo of your prescription or upload the PDF.
2. Our medical AI reads the medications in seconds and shows a confidence score for every line.
3. We find the nearest pharmacy with ALL of your medications in stock — never a partial delivery.
4. A courier prepares and delivers your order; track the ride live on your phone.

Why SAHA Santé?
- Encrypted prescriptions, audited access, strict confidentiality of your health data.
- Real-time stock checks: one pharmacy must have everything, or nothing is sent.
- You confirm before sending: every detected medication can be reviewed.
- Live order tracking with notifications.
- Also available on WhatsApp.

Languages: French, English, Arabic.

Required permissions: camera (to scan the prescription), location (to find the nearest pharmacy and follow the courier), notifications (order tracking).
```
